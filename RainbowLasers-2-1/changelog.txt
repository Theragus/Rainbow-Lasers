Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-09-16
  Changes:
    - Updated for Factorio 2.1. The mod no longer loads in Factorio 2.0.
    - Reuploaded as "RainbowLasers-2-1" because the original "RainbowLasers" entry
      is marked for 2.0 only. Marked incompatible with the original so the two
      cannot be enabled at once.
  Bugfixes:
    - The beam graphics are now located by their light flag instead of by a fixed
      layer index, so the mod no longer recolours the wrong layer when another mod
      adds beam layers.
    - Mods that replace the laser beam sheets with a multi-file or striped sheet no
      longer leave the RGB sheet unused.
    - Loading no longer fails when another mod removes the laser beam prototype or
      its graphics.
  Minor Features:
    - Every body variation of the beam is recoloured, not just the first one.
    - "laser-beam-no-sound" is recoloured as well when another mod adds it.
